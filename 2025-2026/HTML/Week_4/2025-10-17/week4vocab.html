Vocabulary Tags

<div>

Container for html elements

<br>

Used to break lines

class

Space list of the class of the elements

id

Identify a unique element without a page

<img>

Used to show a image in the web

<a>

Create a hyperlink that sent you to another place in the internet

<hr>

Make a horizontal line to separate the content

<p>

Make paragraph of the text

<h1>

Tells people about the page

<span>

Highlight or style parts of text without breaking lines
